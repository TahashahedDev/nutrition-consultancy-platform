# **App Name**: NutriZen

## Core Features:

- Landing Page: Showcase the consultant's expertise and services, highlighting the value proposition.
- About Section: Provide detailed information about the nutrition consultant's background, qualifications, and philosophy.
- Plan Display: Clearly present the 3, 6, and 9-month nutrition plans, including benefits and pricing.
- WhatsApp Integration: Integrate WhatsApp CTA buttons for easy client contact and consultation booking.
- Contact Form: Implement a simple contact form to collect client inquiries.
- Form Submission Handling: Store form submissions in Google Sheets for easy management.
- UPI QR Display: Display a UPI QR image for easy and direct payments.

## Style Guidelines:

- Dark scheme. Primary color: Deep blue (#293462) for trustworthiness. Chosen for its sense of professionalism.
- Background color: Near-black (#121214). To achieve the 'dark mode' look the user requested.
- Accent color: Indigo (#4B5D95) to complement the blue tones with added contrast and vibrancy.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' sans-serif font for a modern and clean look.
- Minimalist layout with a focus on clear and concise content presentation.
- Subtle 3-D card effects and smooth transitions for an enhanced user experience.
- Use simple, modern icons to represent different services and benefits.
